Getting started 开始使用

1. ＋ New item — add each consumable or tool (shampoo bottle, comb, gloves) with its cost per unit and current quantity.
2. Add recipes below — how much of each item one service uses (5% of a bottle = enter 5 with "% of unit").
3. Record services count with sales — the Sales form and the daily-report verify card now have a "services 次数" field.
That's it — this page then shows stock on hand, cost per grooming, margin per service, and when to reorder.
